🏐 Beginner's Guide to Volleyball 🏐

Basic Rules

  • Serve the ball over the net!
  • Only 3 touches of the ball per side!
  • Score up to 25 points with 5 sets. 5th set is up to 15 points!

Volleyball Equipment

  • Volleyball shirt (jersey) and shorts!
  • Kneepads!
  • Sports shoes!
  • A volleyball!

Basic Techniques

  • Passing the ball to either the setter or other side!
  • Setting the ball to the spikers!
  • Spike the ball!
  • Serving the ball!
